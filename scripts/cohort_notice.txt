A short note about your Slab Worthy data, because we are changing something we already told you.

When you graded a comic, our privacy policy said we would keep the photos and grading details for 90 days. We are extending that to 24 months, and it applies to grades you have already submitted — which is why I am writing rather than quietly updating a page.

Why. Professional grading usually takes longer than 90 days. If you send a comic to CGC and it comes back four months later, we want to be able to compare their grade against ours. That comparison is the main way we find out where our grading is wrong. Under a 90-day window we were deleting the data right before it became useful.

What we use it for. Operating the service, checking our grading accuracy, and improving it. Nothing else. We do not sell it or share it, and we will not use your photos in marketing without asking you first.

You still own your photos, and you can still delete them. Reply to this email or write to privacy@slabworthy.com and we will delete your grading submissions — photos included — within 30 days. If you would rather we kept to the original 90 days for your account, say so and we will.

If you are happy either way, there is nothing to do.
