Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 13, 2026

Introduction

Slab Worthy ("we," "us," or "our") is a service provided by CollectionCalc. We're committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle your data.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information when you use slabworthy.com and our related services.

Information We Collect

Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and password (stored securely using industry-standard hashing). You must be at least 16 years old to create an account.

Payment Information: When you subscribe to a paid plan, payment is processed by Stripe. We do not collect, store, or have access to your full credit card number. Stripe provides us with limited billing information (last 4 digits of your card, expiration date, billing address) for transaction records.

Comic Images: When you use our assessment feature, you upload photos of your comics. These images are processed by our AI to identify the comic and assess condition. Images are also screened for prohibited content using automated image analysis (see "Image Processing and Third-Party Services" below). Images you submit for grading are retained for a limited period for quality and diagnostic purposes — including grades you do not save to a collection — as described under "Grading Data & Image Retention" below.

Slab Guard Registration Data: When you register a comic with Slab Guard, we generate and store a perceptual hash (digital fingerprint) of your comic's image, along with comic details (title, issue, grade) and a unique serial number. This fingerprint is a mathematical representation — it cannot be used to reconstruct your original photo.

Usage Data: We collect information about how you interact with our service, including comics assessed, valuations viewed, feature usage, and subscription activity. This helps us improve the service.

Technical Data: We automatically collect certain technical information including your IP address, browser type, and device information for security and analytics purposes.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve our comic assessment and valuation services
  • Process your uploaded images through our AI analysis system
  • Send you service-related emails (password resets, important updates)
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve features and user experience
  • Protect against fraud and maintain security

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for money. We do share limited advertising data with Meta, which certain state privacy laws may treat as a "sale" or "share" — see "Advertising & Analytics Cookies" below, including how to opt out.

Image Processing and Third-Party Services

When you upload comic images, they are processed using multiple services. We want to be transparent about exactly what happens to your images:

Content Safety Screening (Amazon Web Services Rekognition): All uploaded images are automatically sent to AWS Rekognition for content moderation. This service analyzes images to detect prohibited content such as explicit material, violence, and other content that violates our policies. AWS Rekognition processes the image and returns labels/categories — it does not store the image after processing. We do not use any facial recognition features. This screening is mandatory and cannot be opted out of; it is required for us to maintain a safe platform and meet our legal obligations.

AI Grading Analysis (Anthropic Claude): Your images are sent to Anthropic's Claude AI for comic identification, condition assessment, and grading estimates. Anthropic processes the image in accordance with their privacy policy and does not use customer data to train their models under their commercial terms.

Image Storage (Cloudflare R2): Uploaded images are stored in Cloudflare R2 cloud storage. Images are associated with your account and are accessible to you, our backend systems, and authorized Slab Worthy staff reviewing grading quality or investigating support requests.

Fingerprint Generation (On Our Servers): For Slab Guard registrations, we generate a perceptual hash on our own servers. This is a one-way mathematical transformation — the hash cannot be reversed to recreate your image.

What we do NOT do with your images:

  • We do not share your images with other users
  • We do not sell your images or image data
  • We do not use facial recognition technology
  • We do not use your images for advertising

You can request deletion of your uploaded images at any time by contacting privacy@slabworthy.com.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate Slab Worthy. Each has their own privacy policy governing how they handle data:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS Rekognition): Automated image content moderation and safety screening. Images are processed in real-time and not retained by AWS after analysis.
  • Anthropic (Claude AI): AI-powered comic identification, condition analysis, and grading estimates.
  • Stripe: Payment processing for paid subscriptions. Stripe handles all credit card data directly — we never see or store your full card number.
  • Cloudflare: Hosting, CDN, and security services.
  • Cloudflare R2: Cloud image storage.
  • Render: Backend API hosting.
  • Resend: Transactional email delivery (password resets, account notifications).
  • Meta Platforms (Facebook/Instagram): Advertising measurement and audience targeting via the Meta Pixel. See "Advertising & Analytics Cookies" below for exactly what this collects and how to opt out.

We require all third-party service providers to handle your data in accordance with applicable privacy laws. We do not sell your personal information for money. Because we share limited advertising data with Meta, some state privacy laws — including the California Consumer Privacy Act — may classify that sharing as a "sale" or "share" of personal information. You can opt out at any time; see "Advertising & Analytics Cookies" below.

Data Retention

Account data: We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal information within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal purposes.

Slab Guard registrations: If you delete your account or downgrade, your Slab Guard registrations are retained in our monitoring database for 90 days. After 90 days, they are permanently deleted. You can request immediate deletion by contacting us.

Grading data and images: See Grading Data & Image Retention below.

If you save a graded comic to My Collection, its photos and grade are retained as part of your collection until you delete the comic, at which point we remove its images and grade data.

Payment records: Transaction records are retained as required by tax and financial regulations (typically 7 years). Stripe retains payment data in accordance with their own retention policies.

Content moderation logs: Records of content safety screening (pass/fail results, not images) are retained for 12 months for security and compliance purposes.

Grading Data & Image Retention

When you grade a comic, we keep the photos you uploaded along with the grade, the sub-grades, and the confidence figures — whether or not you save the comic to your collection.

How long we keep it. Twenty-four months from the date you submitted the grade. We previously said 90 days. We extended it because professional grading services routinely take longer than 90 days to return a result, and the whole point of keeping your submission is to compare our grade against the professional one when it arrives. A 90-day window deleted the data before that comparison was possible.

What we do with it. We use your submitted photos and grading data to operate the service, to evaluate how accurate our grading is, and to improve it. That is the whole of it. We do not sell it, we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes, and we do not use your photos in marketing or publish them without asking you first and separately.

You own your photos. Uploading a comic to Slab Worthy does not transfer ownership of the images to us. You grant us a non-exclusive licence to use them for the purposes described above, and nothing further.

Deleting your data. You can ask us to delete your grading submissions at any time, and we will do so within 30 days. This includes the images themselves, not just the database records — the photos are removed from storage. Write to privacy@slabworthy.com. Deleting your account deletes this data as well.

One exception. If you send us feedback about a specific grade, we may keep that submission longer so we can look into what you told us. If you would rather we did not, say so and we will delete it.

Advertising & Analytics Cookies

We advertise Slab Worthy on Facebook and Instagram. To measure whether those ads actually work, we use the Meta Pixel — a small piece of tracking code from Meta Platforms, Inc. that runs on the pages of this site. This is a third-party advertising and analytics technology, and it is the only one we use.

What the Meta Pixel collects: When you visit a page on slabworthy.com, the pixel sends the following to Meta: the page you viewed and its URL, your IP address, your browser type and version, your device and operating system, screen size, the page that referred you to us, and the identifiers stored in Meta's own cookies (including the _fbp browser cookie the pixel sets on this site, and the fbc click identifier if you arrived from one of our ads).

What events we send: In addition to page views, we tell Meta when two specific things happen — that someone completed our signup form, and that someone completed email verification. We send only the fact that the event happened, so we can tell how many sign-ups an ad produced — never any detail about who you are or what you signed up with. Note that Meta can still associate an event with you through its own cookies and your Facebook or Instagram account; that association is made by Meta, not by us. We do not send Meta your email address, your name, your phone number, your password, your payment details, your comic images, or your grading results. We do not use Meta's Advanced Matching, Conversions API, or any other feature that would transmit your contact details to Meta.

How Meta may use it: Meta may use this data to measure the performance of our ads, to build audiences of people who behave similarly to our visitors ("lookalike audiences"), to show you Slab Worthy ads elsewhere on Facebook and Instagram, and for its own purposes as described in Meta's own privacy policy. Once the data reaches Meta, Meta's Privacy Policy and Business Tools Terms govern what it does with it — we act as independent controllers of that data, and we do not control Meta's own use of it.

This is not a "sale" for money, but it may count as one legally: We do not receive any payment for this data. However, sharing information with an advertising partner for targeted advertising may be treated as a "sale" or a "share" of personal information under certain state privacy laws, including the California Consumer Privacy Act. If you are covered by such a law and want to opt out, use any of the methods below or email us at privacy@slabworthy.com and we will honour the request.

How to opt out: You have several options, and none of them affect your ability to use Slab Worthy:

  • Turn on "Do Not Track" or an equivalent tracking-protection setting in your browser. Safari (Intelligent Tracking Prevention) and Firefox (Enhanced Tracking Protection) block this kind of pixel by default.
  • Block third-party cookies in your browser settings, or use a content blocker or privacy extension — the pixel will not load.
  • Adjust what Meta is allowed to do with data collected about you off its own platform: Meta Ad Preferences and Off-Facebook Activity. Off-Facebook Activity lets you disconnect or clear the activity businesses like ours have sent to Meta about you.
  • Opt out of interest-based advertising generally through the Digital Advertising Alliance or the European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance.
  • Email privacy@slabworthy.com and ask us to stop. We will confirm when it is done.

The Meta Pixel is loaded on the pages of slabworthy.com, including pages you see once you are signed in. It records page views and the two conversion events described above and nothing else — it has no access to your uploaded comic images, your grades, your collection, your Slab Guard registrations, or your payment details, and none of those are ever transmitted to Meta.
